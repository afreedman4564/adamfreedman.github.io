How I Think

Better decisions don’t always require more analytics.

Most operational performance problems are not data problems—they’re decision-system design problems.

I start with the business problem, not the technology. I look for the bottleneck, understand the tradeoffs, and determine what will actually improve the outcome.

Sometimes that means building an advanced analytical model. Sometimes it means redesigning a process. And sometimes the best solution is simply removing a step that doesn’t add value.

The objective isn’t more analytics. It’s better decisions.
