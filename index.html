How I Think

Better decisions don’t always require more analytics.

Most operational performance problems are not data problems. They’re decision system design problems.

I start with the business problem, not the analytics. I look at where decisions break down, what information is available, who owns the decision, and what constraints or tradeoffs shape the outcome.

Sometimes the answer is an advanced model. Sometimes it’s a redesigned process. And sometimes the best solution is removing a step entirely.

The objective isn’t more analytics. It’s better decisions.

Transformation in Action

Turning better decisions into better outcomes.

Case 01

From Proof of Concept to Enterprise Capability

Forecasting variability created an opportunity to test machine learning across the UK and Mexico. The initial proof of concept demonstrated value and became the foundation for a broader Machine Learning Center of Excellence.

43 models deployed in the first 12 months.

Case 02

Simplifying Before Automating

A process step had been added over time and was creating unnecessary complexity. Rather than automate the existing process, I challenged whether the step was needed at all.

The step was eliminated, removing 35 monthly hours of work. It was one of several redesigns contributing to more than 500 hours of monthly effort reduction.

Case 03

Deciding with Imperfect Information

Conflicting forecasting drivers created uncertainty around resource requirements. Rather than wait for perfect information, I aligned stakeholders around the best available forecast and established a path to reforecast as conditions changed.

The decision ultimately supported approximately 30 incremental FTE and $2M in incremental operating expense.
